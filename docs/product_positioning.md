# PracticeLens Product Positioning

PracticeLens is a private practice-review tool for musicians who can already attempt a phrase, riff, or take and want objective feedback before asking other people.

It is not yet positioned as a beginner tutor, transcription engine, DAW, or full music-learning platform.

## Current product promise

PracticeLens helps a musician:

- compare multiple takes against a reference;
- choose the strongest take in a session;
- find a recurring weakness;
- get a concrete next recording target;
- keep a local history of practice sessions;
- review progress privately.

## Best current user

PracticeLens currently fits best when the user:

- already knows the phrase, riff, vocal line, or part;
- can record several complete attempts;
- wants feedback without posting the take publicly or asking friends repeatedly;
- wants a repeatable local practice loop.

## Less suitable today

PracticeLens is less suitable when the user:

- is learning the material from zero;
- cannot yet play the phrase end to end;
- needs fingering, tab, notation, or slow-practice instruction;
- submits long recordings with restarts, unrelated material, or heavy backing-track mismatch;
- expects full polyphonic/chord transcription or human-level musical judgment.

## Relationship to HDE

PracticeLens should remain a focused vertical product.

In the future, it can provide high-level music-practice signals to a Human Development Environment, such as:

- session completed;
- recurring weakness;
- progress improved or declined;
- next practice target;
- confidence level;
- instrument/recording profile.

HDE should consume those signals later. PracticeLens should continue to own music-practice analysis and should not become a general personal-development system.
